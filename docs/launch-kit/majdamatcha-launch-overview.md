# MajdaMatcha Launch Kit Overview

MajdaMatcha is a premium Saudi matcha coffee shop concept built around the idea of Japanese matcha craft served through Saudi hospitality. The launch kit is split into four working deliverables.

## Deliverables

- Brand and menu system: `docs/brand/brand-menu-system.md`
- Saudi market and business plan: `docs/research/saudi-market-business-plan.md`
- Social media launch kit: `docs/social/social-launch-kit.md`
- Website implementation: React/Vite app in `src/`, with assets in `public/assets/`

## Website Direction

The hero section adapts the product-forward structure of royalbev.com: oversized stacked brand type, a central beverage product, airy background, floating natural accents, and smooth motion. It does not copy Royalbev's brand assets or product language.

Core sections:

- Hero: brand signal, launch CTA, central iced matcha product image.
- Menu: signature Saudi-localized matcha drinks with SAR pricing.
- Brand: positioning, store experience, gifting system.
- Plan: business assumptions, margins, AOV targets, roadmap.
- Social: launch content pillars and platform energy.

## Launch Thesis

MajdaMatcha should launch Riyadh-first as a matcha-first Saudi hospitality brand. The commercial route should begin with pop-ups and creator tastings, progress into a delivery-led micro-cafe or shop-in-shop, then expand toward a compact flagship once repeat demand and margins are proven.

## Brand Pillars

- Matcha quality and clear preparation standards.
- Saudi hospitality through flavor, service, and gifting.
- Premium visual product made for social sharing.
- Retail and catering from day one, not as an afterthought.
