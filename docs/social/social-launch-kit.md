# MajdaMatcha Saudi Arabia Social Launch Kit

## Launch Objective

Position MajdaMatcha as the elegant, feel-good matcha ritual for Saudi customers who want clean energy, beautiful daily routines, and cafe-quality taste at home or on the go.

Primary launch markets: Riyadh, Jeddah, Khobar/Dammam, and online KSA-wide.

Primary audience:
- Women and men ages 18-35 who follow wellness, cafes, beauty, fitness, study/work routines, and premium food trends.
- Cafe explorers, matcha beginners, Gen Z students, young professionals, creators, and gift buyers.
- Arabic-first and bilingual audiences who respond to polished visuals, trustworthy product education, and local cultural cues.

Core promise:
MajdaMatcha brings calm, clean energy to the Saudi day: smooth taste, elevated ritual, and a modern green moment worth sharing.

Tone:
Warm, modern, premium, playful in small doses, and culturally aware. Use English as the base with light Arabic phrases such as "yalla", "sabah al khair", "ahlan", "bismillah", "jalsah", "habibi/habibti", and "sahtain" where natural.

## Platform Positioning

Role: Brand world, product education, aspirational lifestyle, creator proof, launch updates, and saved content.

Content style:

## Influencer Collaboration Plan

### Creator Tiers

Nano creators: 2,000-10,000 followers.
Use for authentic UGC, tasting reactions, and niche communities. Prioritize students, gym creators, food reviewers, and cafe explorers.

Micro creators: 10,000-75,000 followers.
Use for conversion-focused reviews, recipe videos, and launch code pushes. Prioritize Riyadh/Jeddah creators with strong story views.

Mid-tier creators: 75,000-300,000 followers.
Use for launch awareness, event attendance, and hero content. Prioritize creators whose visual identity fits premium wellness and cafe culture.

Selective celebrity/high-profile creators:
Use only if they can create credible lifestyle integration. Avoid one-off posts that feel disconnected from the product.

### Creator Categories

- Cafe and food reviewers.
- Wellness, Pilates, gym, and clean lifestyle creators.
- Beauty and "get ready with me" creators.
- Study, productivity, and workday routine creators.
- Saudi lifestyle creators in Riyadh, Jeddah, and Eastern Province.
- Home hosting and gifting creators.

## Visual Direction

Color cues:
- Matcha green as the hero.
- Warm white, soft grey, and glass transparency for premium freshness.
- Accent colors from recipe flavors: strawberry pink, vanilla cream, rose blush, coconut white, date caramel.

Lighting:
- Natural daylight.

Composition:

Track daily during the first 30 days:
- Follower growth by platform.
- Reach and video views.
- Reel/TikTok completion rate.
- Saves, shares, comments, and story replies.
- Waitlist signups.

Success signals:
- At least 3-5 repeatable content formats emerge by day 30.
- Creator content outperforms brand-only content in conversion or saves.
- UGC begins without direct prompting.
- Audience questions shift from "What is matcha?" to "Which one should I buy?"

## Launch Guardrails

- Do not make medical, detox, weight-loss, or disease-related claims.
- Use "calm energy", "daily ritual", and "smooth taste" instead of exaggerated health promises.
- Confirm any ingredient, caffeine, origin, shipping, or certification claims before posting.
- Keep Arabic phrases natural and light; avoid forced translation.
- Respect local norms in styling, music, event footage, and creator selection.
- Get written usage rights for creator content before running it as paid ads.
